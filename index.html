The practice

A simple meditation you can learn in one sitting.

Twin Heart Meditation is done sitting in a chair, usually for about twenty minutes. You listen and follow along. There is nothing to memorise, nothing to recite, and you do not need to sit on the floor.

It is practised in universities, workplaces and community rooms in many countries. You do not need any particular background to take part.

Length

A session

What happens when you sit

Four quiet steps. You are guided through each one; there is nothing to prepare in advance.

  1. 01

    Settle in

    A few minutes of gentle stretching and slow breathing, so the body can loosen.

  2. 02

    The guided part

    Spoken instructions keep your attention on the breath and on goodwill toward the people around you.

  3. 03

    Quiet

    A stretch of silence, where the mind is left to rest, not pushed anywhere.

  4. 04

    Finishing

    We close slowly, with a few minutes to sit before heading back into the day.

Why people come

What regular practitioners notice

Experiences differ. These are the changes people most often describe after Twin Heart Meditation.

  • Students

    A settled mind before study or exams: longer concentration, reading that sticks.

  • Working professionals

    A steadier footing through stress and anxiety, and clearer thinking at work and at home.

  • Everyone else

    Better sleep, a calmer temper, a little more patience with the people around you.
